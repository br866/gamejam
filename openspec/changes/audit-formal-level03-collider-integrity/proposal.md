## Why

Formal Level 3's broad automatic collider proxies may not accurately represent all retained visuals and can create unintended blocking volumes. A full collider audit is required to remove confirmed physical defects while preserving the curated art and valid traversal foundation.

## What Changes

- Audit every FormalLevel03 Collider for invalid dimensions, duplicate coverage, unsupported placement, inappropriate visual-only coverage, and route/spawn interference.
- Correct confirmed defective scene-owned collider proxies without changing curated renderers or their transforms.
- Validate the Pad-adjacent spawn area and formal movement route after correction.

## Capabilities

### New Capabilities
- `formal-level03-collider-integrity`: Defines valid and non-disruptive Collider coverage for the retained Formal Level 3 layout.

### Modified Capabilities
- None.

## Impact

- Affects scene-owned Collider proxies in FormalLevel03 and its manifest.
- Does not modify the source scene, visual curation, player prefabs, controls, or mechanics.
