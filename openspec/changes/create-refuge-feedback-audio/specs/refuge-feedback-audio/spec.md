# Refuge Feedback Audio

## Requirements

### Requirement: Safe area and checkpoint share a sonic family

The two one-shots SHALL share a soft filtered whoosh and restrained shimmering
high-frequency layer without sounding like a mechanical pressure plate or a
bright fantasy reward.

#### Scenario: safe area activates

- **WHEN** the cooperative safe-area condition completes
- **THEN** the sound presents a light, airy confirmation with a gentle tail

#### Scenario: checkpoint activates

- **WHEN** a checkpoint records progress
- **THEN** the sound presents the same identity with a stronger low-mid
  confirmation and slightly fuller shimmer
