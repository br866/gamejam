## ADDED Requirements

### Requirement: Level 5 controlled-route traversal
Formal Level 5 SHALL provide supported, clear traversal from its entry anchors through the controlled escape corridor and into the final room, including collision and navigation boundaries that preserve the intended player and monster routes.

#### Scenario: Traverse the Level 5 route
- **WHEN** the player pair progresses through the Level 5 escape and final-room route
- **THEN** each required player path is supported, free of unintended blocking overlap, and consistent with the configured monster access boundaries
