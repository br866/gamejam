## Purpose

Allow the persistent formal human and dog actors to enter Formal Level 4 on stable foundational collision without duplicating player ownership.

## ADDED Requirements

### Requirement: Grounded Formal Level 4 entry
The system SHALL provide separate human and dog entry anchors on enabled supporting collision within the Level 4 environment.

#### Scenario: Formal Level 4 loads through FormalPersistent
- **WHEN** FormalPersistent loads FormalLevel04
- **THEN** exactly one persistent human/dog pair is placed at separate grounded Level 4 entry anchors without blocking overlap.
